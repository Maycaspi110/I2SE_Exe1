1. Purpose. The system computes 2D cross-section images of a 3D volumetric scan (NRRD format) along arbitrary planes defined by the user, and exports them as a video, one frame per plane.

2. Users. Radiologists, physicians, medical researchers and developers of medical imaging software.

3. Usage. The system is a command-line tool: python Ex0.py <volume.nrrd> <planes.csv> <output.mp4>. There is no graphical interface.
